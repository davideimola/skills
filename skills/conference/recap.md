# After: the Recap

## 1. Gather

Fold `## Da smistare` into Takeaways first ([capture.md](capture.md)). Then read `## Obiettivi`, `## Agenda` and
`## Takeaways`. Ask me in one message: any Session I skipped or swapped, anything I never wrote down.

## 2. Write the Recap

Under `## Recap`, in Italian, written for me rereading it in three months:

```markdown
### In breve
<3-5 lines: what the Conference was worth against my Obiettivi, honestly>

### Temi
- **<theme>**: <what I heard across Sessions, with the Sessions linked by title>

### Follow-up
#### Da approfondire
- [ ] <item> · <link or Session>
#### Da provare
- [ ] <item> · <link or Session>
#### Da contattare
- [ ] <person> · <why> · <where to find them>
```

Themes come from recurring Takeaways, never from abstracts alone: the Recap is what I took away, not what
was on stage. A `condividere` Takeaway that never became a post goes under its theme.

Done when every Takeaway is reflected in a theme or a follow-up. Show it to me, iterate, write it, then set
`#status=done`.

## 3. Hand-offs

Offer each one once, in a single closing message; do only what I accept:

- **Content**: a Takeaway that could become a blog post, a LinkedIn post or a Talk becomes a spark through
  the `/idea` skill.
- **A Session worth keeping**: one I will come back to beyond this Conference becomes a permanent note in
  Note `K6y26bPbWdII` (label `type` = `note`, relation `template` = `Hyp0RFyfK63n`), linked from the Recap.
- **Real work**: a follow-up big enough to be a Project is mine to create.
- **For the team**: a version of the Recap to present at work, generated in chat only. It stays out of
  Trilium: my Trilium holds no work notes.
- **A deck**: on request, an sli.dev deck from the Recap, in a folder I choose outside
  `davideimola/presentations` (that repo is public and holds my own Talks).

Done when I have answered each offer.
