# Davide's skills

The personal skills Davide uses to run his writing and his public work, all backed by his Trilium notes.

## Content

**Talk**:
A talk Davide gives himself, tracked as a content topic on the talk channel.
_Avoid_: Session (for Davide's own talks)

**Queue**:
The steady lane of 2-3 short posts a week, drafted from material Davide already has and planned ahead.
_Avoid_: Schedule, backlog

**Live lane**:
Short posts born from something happening now: a conference, a person met, a reaction.
_Avoid_: Real-time posts

## Conferences

**Conference**:
An event Davide goes to, as an attendee, a speaker, or both at once.
_Avoid_: Conf, event (when meaning a conference)

**Session**:
One item on a Conference's program, given by someone else.
_Avoid_: Talk (reserved for Davide's own talks), intervention

**Agenda**:
The Sessions Davide picks to follow at a Conference.
_Avoid_: Watchlist, box, personal schedule

**Takeaway**:
Something Davide carries away from a Conference, from a Session or a hallway, with a kind: `approfondire` (dig into), `provare` (try), `condividere` (share) or `contattare` (reach a person).
_Avoid_: Spunto, note, insight, spark (a spark is a content idea; a Takeaway can become one)

**Recap**:
Davide's own written summary of a Conference: what he saw, the recurring themes, and the follow-ups.
_Avoid_: Report, summary
